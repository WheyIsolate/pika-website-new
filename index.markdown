## Live Here!
          
**We will pay your cancellation fee!**

If you live here Fall 2026, rent wil be **$4500** for the semester, including all your housing and food needs (utilities, mealplan, groceries, toiletries, etc.). This is ~40% of dorm housing + MIT mealplan! (depending on dorm/mealplan). We achieve low rent and food costs through communal labor.

Per MIT policy, pika houses only MIT students (undergrad and grad) during the academic year, but summers are open to the wider community! Summer rent varies depending on number of roomates, but averages to less than **$3000** for the summer.

If you are interesting in living about pika, read more [about applying](/getinvolved)

*We will pay all undergraduate cancellation fees, and we can help with graduate cancellation fees, but we won't for example pay multiple months of full rent.
        

## Mealplan
          
Dinner served **every day!** Your first quarter is free!!! Read [about mealplan](/mealplan) and email [yfnkm@mit.edu](mailto:yfnkm@mit.edu) to sign up!
